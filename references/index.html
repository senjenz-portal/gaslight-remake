Reference Gallery

The images behind the three.js scene

Every visual in the experience traces to one of these. The style master and plates were generated with NB Pro (gemini-3-pro-image) using the locked low-poly diorama prompt template; the 3D assets were generated by Scenario — a concept image first (flux, same style template), then image→3D (model_yvo3d-image-to-3d). Raw candidates and sha256 manifests live in the project's immutable assets/raw/ archive.

1 · The style master

The single reference every other asset — and the three.js lighting, palette and camera mood — was matched against: Prussian-blue night, amber window and hearth, faceted Victorian figures, flat-shaded chunky low poly.

# Sign-off rubric — Gaslight Remake (Fable-max review, 3 lenses)

Reviewed each round from full-lap Playwright screenshots + file/code audit.
Sign-off = zero MAJOR findings on all three lenses in the same round.

## 1. Visual
- Low-poly diorama aesthetic: faceted, flat-shaded, floating on dark rock base,
  Prussian-blue night + amber window/lamp glow (the locked style family).
- Generated 3D assets integrated: consistent scale, palette-coherent, no
  texture smearing/z-fighting; silhouettes read at diorama distance.
- Typography: cream serif in the dark margin, edgeless, <=3 lines/unit.
- Every shot compositionally intentional; focus target on-frame, unoccluded.

## 2. Engaging
- The scene PERFORMS (pantomime motion on a beat clock), never a static frame.
- Interactions are diegetic verbs: click to advance prose, press-and-hold to
  inspect the note (watermark reveal-with-progress), completing action turns
  the page.
- Audio: ambience bed + moment cues (fire, page/paper, hoofbeats, door) keyed
  to beats; no dead silence, no repetitive loop fatigue over one lap.
- Pacing: no unit overstays; freeze frames hold tension, not boredom.

## 3. User / clarity (the "much more clear" mandate)
- A first-time reader who has never heard of this story can state after the
  lap: who Holmes/Watson are, that an anonymous grand client sent an undated
  note, the watermark clue, that a visitor arrives. (Beat-I facts.)
- Affordances discoverable in <5s without instructions (breathing cue on
  clickables; hold-verb visibly hinted).
- Text legible at 1x on a laptop and iPad-portrait ratio; contrast >= WCAG AA.
- No wedge states: spam-clicking never breaks; every unit reachable and
  re-playable; progress always visible (page/unit indicator).

# THE BAR — Fable's sign-off criteria for the 3D sets (supervisor/reviewer: Fable 5; implementors: Opus 5)

A set passes when ALL of these hold. Fable reviews every round with named
defects; rounds repeat until zero majors. "Lap clean" is not sign-off.

1. SAME PLACE, SAME MOOD — side-by-side vs the painted plate at the book's
   framing (compare.jpg, same camera): a reader must say "that is the same
   place." Composition landmarks in position; the plate's light story
   (moonpath, fire warmth, dawn shaft) recognizably present.
2. NO BLOCKOUT SURVIVORS — every visible object has been through form AND
   material passes: no unsculpted blobs, no untextured primitives, no
   scattered-quad water. The pass LOG (per-pass render + judgment vs the
   plate) is the proof; a set without its log is an automatic fail.
3. THE WATER LAW — water is a surface: wine-dark base, coherent emissive
   moonpath band with animated seeded sparkle, swell, foam at shorelines.
   Square white confetti is the named failure.
4. CRAFT DENSITY AT THE CAVE'S LEVEL — the cave set (createCaveScene.js)
   is the shipped bar: prop count, silhouette richness, material variety
   per square meter must be comparable, judged by eye on the compare sheet.
5. STORY INTEGRITY — the 81-unit walk passes on the rebuilt sets: all
   gates by their verbs, posture/obstacle/determinism laws green, zero
   console errors, per-beat screenshots refreshed.
6. FABLE'S EYE, FINAL — screenshots reviewed by Fable personally each
   round; majors block, minors are logged with the round number. The
   owner's eye outranks Fable's; Fable's outranks every instrument.

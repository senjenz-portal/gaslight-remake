Sequence-level problems:

- **Frames 1 and 3 are god-view geography.** Frame 1 explains the island but withholds human scale; frame 3 is labeled an approach yet already feels like an interior inventory shot. Move frame 3 behind the crew and let the cave entrance engulf them.
- **Frame 8 is the closest thing to conventional dialogue coverage**, but the bright foreground giant edge nearly burns out and the centered subject has weak look-room. Offset the speaker and preserve the same axis established in frame 5.
- The sequence needs a consistent visual rule: **human scenes at human eye level; giant encounters from below; giant POVs looking oppressively downward.** Currently the camera often floats at neutral diorama height, so the Cyclops rarely towers.
- Depth of field should reveal information through racks—bowl to giant, auger tip to eye, fleece to hidden man—not simply blur large foreground obstructions.
